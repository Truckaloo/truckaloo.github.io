Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Truckaloo Studio ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Truckaloo (@TruckalooKids). It is not available to the public.

What data the app uses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to: read the owner's own channel information, video list and statistics; and upload videos to the owner's own channel, which are uploaded as private until the owner decides to publish them.

What the app does not do

The app does not collect data about viewers, does not collect any personal information from children, does not show ads, does not sell or share any data with third parties, and does not access any other person's Google account.

Storage and deletion

Access credentials are stored privately by the channel owner and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy. YouTube data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Contact

Questions: use the contact information on the Truckaloo YouTube channel's About page.
