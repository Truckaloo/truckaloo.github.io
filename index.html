Truckaloo Studio

Truckaloo Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Truckaloo (@TruckalooKids), which makes short, original 3D animated stories for young children starring Pip the yellow pickup truck and friends.

The tool helps the channel owner upload the channel's own videos and read the channel's own statistics through the YouTube API. It is not offered to other users.
